# Data retention, privacy and safeguarding policy

The school is the data controller and must document its lawful basis before collecting learner data. Access follows least privilege and welfare/conduct information is restricted to authorized safeguarding roles. Staff must not export or share learner data through personal accounts or devices.

## Data minimization and retention

- Collect only information necessary for attendance, learning and safeguarding.
- Attendance and academic records follow the school/statutory schedule approved by the data controller.
- Audit evidence defaults to seven years; delivered/failed communications default to one year.
- Expired sessions, device sessions and reset tokens are removed automatically.
- Welfare and conduct records require a documented schedule based on safeguarding and legal obligations; they must not be deleted while a hold, active case or statutory requirement applies.
- Backups inherit the same classification and expire after 30 days unless retained for an active recovery investigation.

## Rights and incidents

Identity must be verified before access, correction, restriction or deletion requests are processed. Corrections remain auditable. Suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, safeguarding disclosures or data leakage must be escalated immediately to the designated safeguarding lead and data-protection officer. Preserve evidence, revoke affected sessions/devices, assess notification duties and document decisions.

## Safeguarding rules

The platform must never diagnose, automate punishment or expose confidential notes to unauthorized staff. High-severity concerns require human review and established school escalation procedures. Emergency safeguarding action is handled by trained people under applicable Ugandan law and school policy—not by software automation.
